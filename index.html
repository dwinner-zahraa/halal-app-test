The Story of H-scan

Hello! I am Zahraa, the creator of H-scan.

While traveling abroad, I experienced a terrible moment: accidentally consuming food that contained non-Halal ingredients. Even though I was using a scanner app at the time, a second check after eating revealed the item was haram after showing as Halal during the first scan!

To make matters worse, many scanners restrict basic usage behind paywalls or limited scan quotas. As a Muslim, I felt every individual deserves effortless, transparent access to reliable Halal verification—100% free, without anxiety or financial barriers.

That realization inspired me to build H-scan for our global community. Your usage means the world to me, and any contributions directly support continuous app maintenance and humanitarian relief. Thank you for being part of this journey!
